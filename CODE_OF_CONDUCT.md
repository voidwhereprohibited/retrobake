# Code of Conduct

RetroBake is a friendly project for people who love retro games, whatever their skill level.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome here, regardless of age, background, experience, identity or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give helpful, respectful feedback on ideas and code.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect other people's time, work and privacy.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting copyrighted material you do not have the rights to share.
- Sexual, hateful or violent content.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through a GitHub security advisory or by contacting @voidwhereprohibited. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban anyone who breaks these rules. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
